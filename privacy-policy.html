2. Data Controller vs. Data Processor Distinction

Under applicable international data protection standards—including the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act 2023), the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the UK GDPR—the legal role of Visitsly depends on the category of data being processed:

  • Visitsly as a Data Controller: Deific Digital acts as an independent Data Controller for the personal data of our direct Customers (e.g., account owners, agency managers, and authorized agent seats), including billing details, login credentials, workspace configuration, and technical logs generated during platform usage.
  • Visitsly as a Data Processor (Service Provider): With respect to Customer-of-Customer Data (end-consumer property leads, prospective tenants, buyers, and inquiries who interact with your connected WhatsApp Business number, Meta Lead Ads, or CRM forms), our Customer (your real estate brokerage or agency) is the sole Data Controller. Visitsly processes lead data strictly on the instructions and on behalf of our Customer in our capacity as a Data Processor / Tech Provider.

If you are an individual whose contact details or WhatsApp messages were captured by a real estate brokerage utilizing Visitsly, please direct your privacy queries or data deletion requests to that specific brokerage directly. We will cooperate with our Customers to fulfill all verified data subject requests.

3. Data We Collect & Receive

We collect and process the following categories of data strictly to deliver our real estate CRM and automation service:

3.1 Account & Workspace Data

  • Personal & Business Identifiers: Full name, business email address, phone number, company/brokerage name, business physical address, and assigned workspace role (e.g., Admin, Agent, Team Member).
  • Authentication Credentials: Securely salted and hashed passwords (we never store plain-text passwords) and multi-factor authentication metadata.

3.2 Customer-of-Customer Data (Lead Data)

When you use Visitsly to manage your real estate pipeline, we process data submitted by or collected from your leads on your behalf:

  • Lead contact information (first and last name, WhatsApp phone number, email address).
  • Inquiry details, property preferences (budget range, preferred Dubai/UAE or local communities, bedroom count, ready-to-move vs. off-plan interest).
  • WhatsApp message history and inbound/outbound chat transcripts exchanged between the lead and your connected WhatsApp Business Account.
  • Scheduled property site visits, site inspection timestamps, and assigned agent notes.

3.3 Meta Platform Data (Facebook Login & WhatsApp Embedded Signup)

To enable automated WhatsApp conversations, lead qualification, and campaign broadcasts, Visitsly participates as a registered Tech Provider using Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API). When you connect via Meta Embedded Signup ("Connect with Facebook"), we receive and store:

  • WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) ID: The unique identifier of your Meta WhatsApp Business account.
  • Phone Number ID & WABA Display Name: The registered WhatsApp Business phone number ID and verified display name.
  • Encrypted System User Access Tokens: OAuth and system tokens required to transmit and receive messages on your behalf. These tokens are encrypted at rest using AES-256 and are never sent to or visible in client-side browser code.
  • Webhook Event Payloads: Real-time message status updates (e.g., sent, delivered, read, failed) and inbound messages initiated by prospects to your WhatsApp Business number.
⚠️ Facebook Password Protection Guarantee

Visitsly NEVER sees, requests, captures, logs, or stores your personal Facebook account password. Authentication and permission authorization occur entirely within Meta's secure OAuth dialogue dialog.

3.4 Google Calendar Integration Data

If you optionally connect Google Calendar to Visitsly to streamline physical property site visits, we access your calendar data using Google APIs:

  • We read event start/end times and busy/free status solely to determine agent availability and avoid double-booking site inspections.
  • We create, update, or remove calendar entries exclusively for confirmed real estate site visits initiated through Visitsly.
  • Google user data is never used for advertising, profiling, or transferred to third parties other than necessary infrastructure sub-processors.

3.5 Billing & Payment Information

Subscription payments for Visitsly software plans are processed through certified, PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant third-party payment gateways (e.g., Stripe, Inc. / Razorpay Software Pvt. Ltd.). Visitsly does NOT collect, handle, or store credit or debit card numbers, card security codes (CVV/CVC), or bank account credentials. We receive only billing tokens, invoice IDs, subscription status, and billing addresses for tax compliance.

3.6 Technical, Log & Cookie Data

When accessing Visitsly, our servers automatically record standard internet telemetry: IP address, device hardware model, browser type, operating system version, referring URLs, request timestamps, and strictly necessary session cookies required for authentication and CSRF security.

Visitsly incorporates generative artificial intelligence capabilities to draft instant preliminary WhatsApp responses, qualify buyer budgets, match properties, and suggest follow-up schedules. Regarding AI processing, we guarantee:

  • Commercial Enterprise APIs: AI inference is conducted using commercial enterprise endpoints (such as OpenAI, LLC or Anthropic, PBC APIs) operating under strict business-tier data confidentiality agreements.

9. Data Retention & Deletion Schedules

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the operational purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is mandated by law:

  • Active Customer Account Data: Maintained for the entire active lifecycle of your Visitsly subscription.
  • WhatsApp Message Logs & Lead Data: Retained within your active workspace according to your CRM settings. Upon account cancellation or deletion request, lead records and message transcripts are permanently purged within 30 calendar days.
  • Meta Tokens & WABA Credentials: Immediately invalidated and purged upon user disconnection or deletion of the Visitsly integration.
  • Statutory Tax & Invoicing Records: Basic invoice records, payment transaction IDs, and company billing names are retained for up to 7 years solely to comply with mandatory statutory tax, auditing, and corporate accounting laws in India. These archives are encrypted, access-restricted, and never utilized for marketing.

10. Data Security & Token Encryption Standards

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of personal data:

  • Token Encryption: All Meta WhatsApp access tokens, webhook signing secrets, and API credentials are encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption. Encryption keys are managed independently via secure Key Management Systems (KMS).
  • Transport Security: 100% of web and API communication is enforced over HTTPS utilizing Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3). Plain-text HTTP is strictly prohibited.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Access to server production environments and databases is restricted to authorized engineers on a strict need-to-know basis, protected by hardware multi-factor authentication (MFA) and audited VPN tunnels.
  • Multi-Tenant Data Isolation: Logical workspace isolation prevents cross-tenant data leaks. Your leads and messages are never visible or accessible to other brokerages.

11. Your Data Protection Rights

Subject to applicable data protection legislation (including the Indian DPDP Act 2023, GDPR, and CCPA), you are entitled to the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold concerning your account.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated personal information.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): Request permanent deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention constraints. Please see our dedicated Data Deletion Instructions page.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Revoke consent previously granted for integrations (such as WhatsApp Business or Google Calendar) at any time.
  • Right to Data Portability: Request an export of your CRM lead records in a structured, machine-readable format (CSV/JSON).
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Submit a formal complaint regarding data handling to our designated Grievance Officer.

To exercise any of these rights, email our privacy team at privacy@visitsly.com. We respond to all verified requests within the statutory timeframe (and no later than 30 calendar days).

12. How to Disconnect WhatsApp & Revoke Meta Permissions

You maintain continuous, unilateral control over Visitsly's access to your WhatsApp Business Account. You may disconnect at any time using either of two methods:

1

In-App Disconnect (Immediate Token Invalidation)

Log in to your Visitsly dashboard, navigate to Settings > Integrations > WhatsApp, and click "Disconnect WhatsApp". This immediately purges all stored access tokens from our active database and unregisters webhooks.

2

Facebook-Side Revocation (Meta Business Integrations)

Log in to your Facebook account, open Settings & Privacy > Settings > Business Integrations (or visit facebook.com/settings?tab=business_tools), locate Visitsly, and click "Remove". This instantly severs all API permissions at the Meta platform level.

For complete self-service instructions, visit our dedicated Data Deletion Instructions page.
