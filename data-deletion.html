This page provides official, binding user data deletion instructions for Visitsly (website: https://visitsly.com), owned and operated by Deific Digital. It fulfills the requirements set forth in the Meta Platform Terms, Meta Developer Policies, and WhatsApp Business Platform Terms for User Data Deletion.

1. Deletion Overview & Commitment

At Visitsly, we believe that users and businesses should have absolute control over their personal data and third-party integrations. If you have connected your Meta (Facebook) account, WhatsApp Business Account (WABA), Google Calendar, or stored lead data in Visitsly, you may request permanent erasure of your data at any time.

We provide multiple simple, transparent, and immediate paths to delete your data: self-service through the Visitsly app dashboard, direct revocation via Facebook Business Integrations settings, direct email request, or our instant online request form below.

2. What Data Is Deleted

Upon receiving and processing a data deletion request, Visitsly permanently deletes the following categories of information from our live databases and cached storage:

  • Meta & WhatsApp Platform Credentials: WhatsApp Business Account ID (WABA ID), Phone Number ID, display phone number, business profile name, webhook signing secrets, and OAuth System User access tokens.
  • Message & Conversation Histories: All inbound and outbound WhatsApp chat transcripts, media attachments, template interaction logs, and webhook delivery status events (sent, delivered, read, failed).
  • Customer-of-Customer Lead Records: Names, phone numbers, email addresses, property preferences, qualification tags, budget records, and agent interaction notes.
  • Third-Party Integration Tokens: Google Calendar authorization tokens, synchronized site-visit calendar mappings, and email dispatch logs.
  • Account Credentials: Password hashes, multi-factor authentication tokens, and user profile metadata (if full workspace deletion is requested).

3. Method 1: In-App Self-Service Deletion (Immediate)

You can delete integration credentials or your entire workspace directly from your Visitsly dashboard at any time without waiting for manual support review:

3.1 Disconnecting WhatsApp Business Only (Purge Meta Data)

1

Log in to Visitsly

Access your dashboard at app.visitsly.com using your account credentials.

2

Navigate to Integrations

From the left sidebar, click Settings > Integrations > WhatsApp Business.

3

Click "Disconnect WhatsApp"

Click the red "Disconnect WhatsApp" button and confirm. This immediately revokes your access tokens, unregisters incoming webhooks from Meta, and wipes all stored WhatsApp API tokens from our active database.

3.2 Complete Workspace & Lead Data Deletion

1

Open Workspace Settings

Navigate to Settings > Workspace > General.

2

Access Danger Zone

Scroll down to the "Danger Zone" section at the bottom of the page.

3

Confirm Workspace Deletion

Click "Delete Workspace & All Data". Enter your password or type your workspace name to confirm. All team accounts, leads, listings, and messages are permanently scheduled for immediate cryptographic destruction.

4. Method 2: Facebook-Side Revocation (Meta Platform)

If you prefer to revoke Visitsly's access directly from Facebook or if you no longer have access to your Visitsly dashboard, you can remove the integration directly via Facebook settings:

1

Log into Facebook

Open Facebook in your browser using the account managing your Meta Business Manager.

2

Open Business Integrations

Navigate to Settings & Privacy > Settings > Business Integrations, or visit direct link: https://www.facebook.com/settings?tab=business_tools.

3

Find & Remove Visitsly

Locate Visitsly in the list of active integrations and click "Remove".

4

Confirm Deletion of Associated Data

Check the box that states: "Delete posts, videos or events Visitsly posted on your timeline and associated business data", and click "Remove". Meta will immediately revoke all access tokens and dispatch an automatic data-deletion callback notification to Visitsly's API endpoint.

5. Method 3: Direct Email Request

You may also submit a data deletion request by emailing our dedicated Privacy & Data Protection Office:

📧 Email Deletion Instructions

To: privacy@visitsly.com
Subject: Data Deletion Request - [Your Workspace or Company Name]
Include in your message:

  • Your registered Visitsly account email address.
  • Your business/brokerage name and workspace URL.
  • Your connected WhatsApp Business phone number or Phone Number ID (if applicable).
  • Specific scope: (e.g., "Full Workspace Deletion", "Disconnect WhatsApp Integration Only", or "Delete Specific Lead Records").

Our security team will acknowledge your email within 7 business days with a formal Reference Tracking Number and complete full deletion within 30 calendar days.

7. Timelines, Reference ID & Confirmation SLA

Deific Digital processes all user data deletion requests in accordance with strict Service Level Agreements (SLAs):

  • Immediate Invalidation: Once triggered through our self-service portal or Facebook Business Integrations, Meta access tokens are invalidated immediately.
  • Acknowledgment & Reference Number: All requests received via email or our online form are acknowledged within 7 business days with a unique Reference Tracking ID (format: DEL-2026-XXXXXX).
  • Complete Permanent Purge: Full data sanitization across all production databases, replicas, and temporary caches is completed within 30 calendar days of request verification.
  • Completion Certificate: Upon final execution, an automated confirmation receipt is sent to the account owner's email address confirming completion.

8. Data Retention Exceptions (Legal & Tax Compliance)

In strict compliance with applicable statutory laws, certain limited records cannot be deleted immediately upon request:

  • Statutory Invoices & Tax Records: Under Indian taxation regulations (including the Goods and Services Tax Act) and applicable international commercial codes, financial transaction records, tax invoices, and payment processor transaction IDs must be retained for up to 7 years.
  • Isolation: Retained billing archives are encrypted, segregated from active operational systems, access-controlled strictly to certified finance personnel, and never used for marketing, profiling, or machine learning.
